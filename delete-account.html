Delete Your Account or Data

AI Sales Pulse app, by Sales Pulse AI, LLC

This page explains how to delete your AI Sales Pulse account (the Pulse app on the web, iPhone and Android), or only some of your data, and what happens to it.

Delete your account

  1. Email support@aisalespulse.com from the email address you use to log in to AI Sales Pulse, with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. We reply to confirm the request came from you. If your company manages your Pulse login, we also let your company's admin know.
  3. Your account is deleted within 30 days, and we email you when it is done. If you want a copy of your data first, ask in the same email, or use Settings > Privacy > Export My Data in the app before you write to us.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number and login details.
  • Your sessions, devices, settings and notification preferences.
  • Connections you made: WhatsApp, Outlook or Gmail access tokens are revoked and removed.
  • If your company closes its whole Pulse account: all of its business data (customers, products, orders, messages, notes and uploaded files) is scheduled for deletion within 30 days.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Your company's shared business records. When one user leaves a company that keeps using Pulse, the customers, orders and conversations belong to the company and stay with it, so the next salesperson can pick up the accounts.
  • Security audit log. A record of logins and account changes is kept as long as the law requires.
  • Backups. Deleted data disappears from backups as they expire in the normal backup cycle.

Delete only some of your data

You do not have to delete your account to remove data:

  • Delete individual records (customers, notes, follow-ups, uploaded files) inside the app.
  • Disconnect WhatsApp, Outlook or Gmail in Settings > Integrations, and mark any chat as personal so Pulse never reads it.
  • Or email support@aisalespulse.com and tell us what to remove. We confirm within 30 days.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
