What it does

A Sales Rep’s Brain, Always On

Four things it does the moment you connect it. No data entry, ever.

Never miss a sale

Pulse watches every account for you. Who is due to reorder, who has gone quiet, and what you promised and never sent. Each one arrives with the message already written.

Know what to sell

It matches what is sitting in your warehouse to what each customer actually buys. Stock worth moving, an open vendor rebate, a promo that fits them. You have the offer before they ask.

Know every account

Every WhatsApp and every email, read and remembered. What they buy, how often, what they turned down last time, and what they still owe. Ask a question, get the answer from your own book.

Manage the whole team

Every rep’s numbers, which follow-ups are getting done and which are slipping, and what the AI costs. Team chat is built in, so the whole floor works in one place.

It hears voice notes too

Customers talk prices into voice memos. Pulse transcribes every business voice note automatically, catches the promise inside it, and translates it to English in one click. Nothing said out loud gets lost anymore.
Voice notes in business conversations only. Personal chats are never transcribed, never even downloaded.

Private stays private

Mark any chat personal and Pulse never reads it, not for alerts, not in search, not for the manager. Your customers belong to the business; your life stays yours.
